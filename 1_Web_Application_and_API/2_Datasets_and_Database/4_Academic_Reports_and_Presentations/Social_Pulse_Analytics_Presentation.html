TAB 3 • FEATURE ASSOCIATIONS & COLLINEARITY

Tab 3: Correlation Matrix (Pearson vs. Spearman)

Spotting strong relationships and collinearity before training models

Likes & Followers (r = +0.88)

Strong positive linear relationship. Accounts with high followers consistently capture higher aggregate likes.

Followers & Engagement (r = -0.31)

Crucial negative correlation! As follower count balloons, community engagement % drops (audience dilution effect).

Pearson vs. Spearman Dual Engine

Pearson tests straight-line trends; Spearman evaluates rank-order monotonicity (Spearman ρ = -0.51 for Followers vs Engagement).

TAB 4 • SUPERVISED MACHINE LEARNING

Tab 4: Regression Models (OLS in Action)

Fitting linear equations: Simple Linear Regression and Multiple OLS

Model A: Screen Time -> Likes

Likes = 9.20(ScreenTime) + 159.67 (R² = 0.0024). Fails completely! Simply scrolling for hours has zero predictive power over creative success.

Model B: Weekly Posts -> Followers

Followers = 651.81(Posts) + 1019.95 (R² = 0.1582). Statistically significant slope: each additional post adds ~652 followers.

Model C: Multiple OLS Regression

Engagement = 9.14 - 0.012(ST) - 0.124(Posts) - 0.00007(Followers). Multiple R² = 0.1389 | Adjusted R² = 0.1346.

The Lesson of Adjusted R²

Explains why Adjusted R² penalizes adding useless predictors, protecting models from overfitting.

TAB 6 • INFERENTIAL STATISTICS (α = 0.05)

Tab 6: Hypothesis Testing (Real Facts vs. Luck)

Parametric tests: Welch's t-test, Paired t-test, and One-Way ANOVA

Weekend vs. Weekday Screen Time

Paired t-Test: t = 36.74, p < 0.0001 (Reject H₀). Weekend usage is significantly higher by +0.77 hours. Overwhelming proof!

Screen Time vs. Engagement Rate

Welch's Two-Sample t-Test: t = -0.595, p = 0.552 (Fail to Reject H₀). High screen time does not statistically alter engagement.

Post Format Performance

One-Way ANOVA: F = 27.10, p < 0.001 (Reject H₀). Statistically proves format drives engagement (Video > Carousel > Photo > Text).

Platform Screen Time Differences

One-Way ANOVA: F = 11.83, p < 0.001 (Reject H₀). Screen time differs significantly by platform (TikTok/Instagram command highest attention).

TAB 6 (PART 2) • DATA CLEANING & ANOMALIES

Tab 6: Anomaly & Outlier Detection

Parametric Z-Score (|Z| > 2.5) vs. Non-Parametric Tukey IQR Fences

The Outlier Hazard in ML

Social datasets contain viral bots and mega-influencers that drag regression lines and ruin generalizability.

Tukey's IQR Fence Method

Bounds: [Q1 - 1.5*IQR, Q3 + 1.5*IQR]. Identifies 116 posting frequency outliers and 79 follower outliers without assuming normality.

Parametric Z-Score Masking

Z-Score flags only 19 post outliers! Why? Extreme values inflate the sample standard deviation (σ), widening the acceptance window.

Core Takeaway for Students

Demonstrates why robust statistics (IQR, Median Absolute Deviation) are essential for real-world ML preprocessing.
